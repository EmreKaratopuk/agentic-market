# Return and Refund Policy

## Return Eligibility

Buyers may return most products within **30 days of the delivery date**. To be eligible for a return, the item must meet all of the following conditions:

- The item is in its original, unused condition
- Original packaging, tags, and accessories are included
- The return is initiated through the marketplace return portal (not directly with the seller)
- The return request is submitted within the 30-day window

### Non-returnable Items

The following items are not eligible for return:

- Perishable goods (food, plants, flowers)
- Digital products, software licences, or gift cards once activated or redeemed
- Customised, personalised, or made-to-order items
- Intimate apparel and swimwear for hygiene reasons
- Hazardous materials
- Items explicitly marked as non-returnable on the product listing

## How to Initiate a Return

1. Log into your account and go to **My Orders**
2. Select the order containing the item you wish to return
3. Click **Request Return** and select the reason from the dropdown
4. A prepaid return shipping label will be emailed within 2 business hours
5. Print the label and drop the package at any approved carrier location within 7 days
6. Track your return using the return tracking number provided

Returns sent without the prepaid label or to an incorrect address cannot be processed.

## Seller Responsibility vs Marketplace Responsibility

### Seller is responsible when:
- The item received does not match the listing description
- The item arrived damaged due to poor packaging by the seller
- The wrong item was sent
- The item is defective or faulty

In these cases, the buyer receives a full refund including original shipping costs, and the seller bears the return shipping cost.

### Marketplace is responsible when:
- The item was lost or damaged by an approved carrier
- A system error caused an incorrect charge

In these cases, the marketplace covers the refund and any shipping costs.

### Buyer-initiated returns (change of mind):
- The buyer bears the return shipping cost if the item matches the listing
- A 5% restocking fee may apply for high-value items over R$500

## Refund Timelines

Once the returned item is received and inspected at the fulfilment centre:

| Refund Method | Processing Time |
|---|---|
| Original credit/debit card | 5–10 business days |
| PIX or bank transfer | 2–3 business days |
| Marketplace credit (instant) | Within 24 hours |

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Buyers may choose marketplace credit for a faster resolution.

## Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be issued in the following cases:

- Only part of a multi-item order is returned
- The returned item shows signs of use or missing accessories (deduction of up to 20%)
- A promotional discount was applied to the original purchase

## Exchanges

Direct exchanges are not supported. To exchange an item, initiate a return and place a new order for the desired product. If the returned item qualifies for a full refund, the refund can be applied as marketplace credit to offset the cost of the new purchase.
