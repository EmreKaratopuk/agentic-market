# Seller Policy

## Eligibility Requirements

To sell on the marketplace, sellers must meet the following requirements:

- Be a registered legal entity or individual with a valid tax identification number
- Provide a verified bank account for payment disbursements
- Agree to the marketplace terms of service and seller code of conduct
- Maintain a minimum seller rating of 3.0 out of 5.0 after the first 30 days of operation
- Have a valid business address and contact information on file

## Onboarding Process

New sellers go through a structured onboarding process:

1. Submit registration form with business and tax details
2. Upload required documents (ID, proof of address, tax certificate)
3. Complete identity verification within 5 business days
4. List at least one product to activate the seller account
5. Receive approval confirmation via email before the first sale can be made

Incomplete applications are held for 15 days before being cancelled.

## Listing Rules

Sellers are responsible for the accuracy of all product listings. Requirements include:

- Product titles must be descriptive and under 200 characters
- At least one product image is required; images must be clear and accurately represent the item
- Prices must include all applicable taxes
- Stock quantities must be updated within 24 hours of inventory changes
- Product descriptions must be in Portuguese or English

## Prohibited Categories

The following product categories are not permitted on the marketplace:

- Weapons, firearms, and ammunition
- Illegal substances or drug paraphernalia
- Counterfeit or pirated goods
- Adult content or services
- Live animals
- Hazardous chemicals without proper certification
- Products that infringe on intellectual property rights

Listings in prohibited categories will be removed immediately and may result in account suspension.

## Commission Structure

The marketplace charges a commission on each completed sale:

- Standard commission: 12% of the item sale price
- Electronics and high-value items (over R$500): 10%
- Books and educational materials: 8%
- Shipping fees are not subject to commission

Commissions are deducted automatically before payment disbursement, which occurs every 14 days.

## Account Suspension

Seller accounts may be suspended for the following reasons:

- Seller rating drops below 2.5 for two consecutive evaluation periods
- Repeated cancellations exceeding 5% of total orders in a 30-day window
- Violation of listing rules or prohibited category policies
- Fraudulent activity or misrepresentation of products
- Failure to fulfill orders within the agreed shipping window

Sellers receive a warning email before suspension except in cases of fraud, where immediate suspension applies. Suspended sellers may appeal within 10 business days.
