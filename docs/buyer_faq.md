# Buyer FAQ

## Account and Registration

### How do I create an account?

Visit the marketplace homepage and click "Sign Up." Provide your name, email address, and a password. You will receive a verification email — click the link to activate your account. Registration is free and takes under two minutes.

### How do I reset my password?

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. Enter your registered email address and you will receive a password reset link valid for 24 hours. If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

### Can I have multiple accounts?

No. Each customer may only have one active account. Duplicate accounts are subject to deactivation.

## Orders and Payments

### What payment methods are accepted?

The marketplace accepts the following payment methods:

- Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Elo, American Express)
- Bank transfer (boleto bancário)
- PIX (instant payment)
- Marketplace credit (from refunds or promotions)

Installment payments of up to 12 months are available for purchases over R$100 made with eligible credit cards.

### How do I track my order?

After your order is confirmed, you will receive a tracking code via email. You can also view order status and tracking information by logging into your account and navigating to "My Orders." The tracking page updates automatically as the carrier scans your package.

### Can I change or cancel my order?

Orders can be cancelled within 1 hour of placement if the seller has not yet started processing. After that window, cancellations must go through the return process once the item is delivered. To cancel, go to "My Orders," select the order, and click "Cancel Order."

## Shipping and Delivery

### How long does delivery take?

Delivery times vary by seller and destination. Standard delivery typically takes 5–12 business days. Express options, when available, take 2–4 business days. Estimated delivery dates are shown at checkout.

### What happens if my order is late?

If your order has not arrived by the estimated delivery date, check the tracking page first. If the tracking shows no movement for more than 3 business days, contact support. Orders confirmed lost by the carrier are eligible for a full refund or replacement.

## Returns and Refunds

### How do I return a product?

Products can be returned within 30 days of delivery. To initiate a return, go to "My Orders," select the order, and click "Request Return." You will receive a prepaid return label by email. Once the item is received and inspected, your refund will be processed within 5–10 business days.

### What items cannot be returned?

- Perishable goods
- Digital products or software licenses once activated
- Customised or personalised items
- Items that have been used, washed, or damaged by the buyer

## Support

### How do I contact customer support?

Support is available through the in-app chat, email (support@marketplace.com.br), or phone Monday to Friday, 9:00–18:00 (BRT). Average response time is under 4 hours for chat and email.

### What if I have a problem with a seller?

If you cannot resolve an issue directly with the seller, you can open a dispute through "My Orders." The marketplace will mediate and reach a resolution within 7–14 business days. See the Dispute Resolution Policy for full details.
