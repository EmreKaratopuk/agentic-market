# Shipping SLA

## Standard Delivery Windows

Delivery timeframes are measured in business days from the date of order confirmation (not placement). Weekends and Brazilian national holidays are excluded.

| Destination Region              | Standard Delivery   | Express Delivery  |
|---------------------------------|---------------------|-------------------|
| São Paulo (capital)             | 3–5 business days   | 1–2 business days |
| São Paulo (state, other cities) | 4–7 business days   | 2–3 business days |
| Rio de Janeiro                  | 4–6 business days   | 2–3 business days |
| South (PR, SC, RS)              | 5–8 business days   | 3–4 business days |
| Southeast (MG, ES)              | 5–8 business days   | 3–4 business days |
| Midwest (GO, DF, MT, MS)        | 7–10 business days  | 4–5 business days |
| Northeast (all states)          | 8–12 business days  | 5–7 business days |
| North (all states)              | 10–15 business days | 6–9 business days |

Express delivery is not available for all sellers or all product categories. Availability is shown at checkout.

## Seller Shipping Obligations

Sellers must dispatch orders within the following timeframes after order confirmation:

- Standard orders: within 2 business days
- Express orders: within 1 business day (same day if order placed before 12:00)

Failure to dispatch within these windows constitutes an SLA breach and may affect seller performance scores.

## Carrier Partners

The marketplace works with the following approved carriers:

- Correios (standard and express)
- Jadlog
- Total Express
- Sequoia
- Loggi (São Paulo metro area only)

Sellers must use an approved carrier to qualify for SLA protection and insurance coverage.

## SLA Breach Handling

### What constitutes an SLA breach?

An SLA breach occurs when:

- The seller does not dispatch within the required window
- The carrier fails to deliver within the estimated window due to carrier error
- The package is lost or damaged in transit

### Buyer remedies for SLA breach

If a delivery SLA is breached:

1. The buyer receives an automatic notification and an updated delivery estimate
2. If the new estimate exceeds 5 additional business days, the buyer may cancel for a full refund with no return required
3. If the item arrives more than 10 business days late, the buyer receives a 10% discount voucher on their next purchase automatically

### Seller consequences for SLA breach

- First breach: warning recorded on seller account
- Two breaches within 30 days: 5% penalty applied to next payment disbursement
- Three or more breaches within 30 days: account review and potential suspension

Breaches caused by carrier error (documented by carrier tracking data) do not count against the seller's SLA record.

## Shipping Insurance

All shipments are insured up to the declared item value. In the event of loss or damage, buyers are entitled to a full refund of the item price plus shipping. Claims must be submitted within 15 days of the expected delivery date.
